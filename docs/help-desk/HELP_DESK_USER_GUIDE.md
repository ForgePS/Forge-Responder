# Help Desk — User Guide

The Help Desk is where you report a problem, ask a question or request a feature. Open it from **Communications → Help Desk** in the menu (in My Work too).

## Opening a help request

1. Open **Help Desk** and choose **New request**.
2. Write a short **subject** and pick a **category**: Bug, Question, Account / access, Feature request, Data issue or Other.
3. Pick a **priority**. Use **Urgent** only when operations are affected right now.
4. Describe what happened, what you expected, and the steps to see it. Include record numbers or names if it's about specific data.
5. Attach screenshots or files if they help (up to 5 files, 10 MB each: images, PDFs and text files).
6. Choose **Send request**. You get a request number such as HD-1042.

The request automatically includes your department, your name and email, the page you were on before opening the Help Desk, your browser and screen size, and the app version, so you don't have to type them.

## Following your requests

**My requests** lists everything you've opened. Each request shows its status:

- **Open**: waiting for the help desk to pick it up.
- **In progress**: someone is working on it.
- **Waiting on you**: the help desk needs an answer or more details from you.
- **Resolved**: the help desk thinks it's fixed. Reply if it isn't and the request reopens.
- **Closed**: finished. Reopen it if you need to, or open a new request.

You get an email when the help desk replies or changes the status. Reply inside FORGE Responder (replies to the email aren't read). You can close your own request at any time.

Only you and the help desk can see your requests.

## Who answers

FORGE support sees every request. Your department's administrators (Fire Chief, IT Admin and department admin roles) also see and can answer requests from your department.

## For department administrators

Department administrators get two more tabs on the Help Desk page:

- **Department queue**: every request from your department. Filter by folder (Active, Assigned to me, Unassigned, Waiting on user, Resolved, Closed), category and priority, or search by number, subject or person. Open a request to change its status, assignee, priority or category, reply to the requester, or add an **internal note** that only help desk staff can see.
- **Notifications**: the email addresses that get every new request from your department. FORGE support is always notified as well.

Each request shows an **Email notifications** log with exactly what was emailed and to whom. Every change is recorded in your department's Activity Log.

## Searching help articles

**Help articles** searches the FORGE guides (this guide, FORGE FIELD, Forge AI, Fleet and Inspections). If you can't find an answer, choose **Open a help request**.
