# RMS Module Priority Specification

## Current Priority

Focus on the RMS and department operations side before incident reporting.

## Build Order

### 1. Personnel
Foundation for all other modules.

Required:
- Personnel list
- Employee profile
- Rank
- Shift
- Assignment
- EMS level
- Driver status
- Contact info
- Documents
- Status

### 2. Certifications
Required:
- Certification type
- Issue date
- Expiration date
- Renewal status
- Upload document
- Expiration alerts
- 30/60/90 day dashboard

### 3. Scheduling
Required:
- A/B/C shift calendar
- Daily roster
- Apparatus assignment
- Minimum staffing
- Leave
- Overtime
- Shift trades

### 4. Training
Required:
- Training events
- Attendees
- Instructor
- Hours
- Category
- EMS CE
- Fire training
- Compliance tracking

### 5. Fleet / Apparatus
Required:
- Unit profile
- Status
- Mileage
- Engine hours
- Pump hours
- PM due
- Repair history
- Documents

### 6. Assets
Required:
- Asset inventory
- Serial numbers
- Assigned apparatus/location
- Inspection due
- Maintenance due
- Replacement date

### 7. Documents
Required:
- SOGs
- Policies
- EMS protocols
- Forms
- Uploads
- Search

### 8. Prevention
Required:
- Occupancies
- Inspections
- Violations
- Reinspections
- Preplans
- Hydrants

### 9. Analytics
Required:
- Staffing trends
- Training compliance
- Fleet readiness
- Certification expirations
- Inspection backlog

### 10. Incidents / EMS / Fire Reports
Lower priority.
Build placeholders now. Expand later.
